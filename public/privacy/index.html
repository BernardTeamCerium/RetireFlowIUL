Sharing

We share your information only with the licensed professional(s) assigned to help you and with service providers that help us run this site and send email (for example, our hosting and email delivery providers). We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel and Meta’s Conversions API to measure how well our Facebook and Instagram ads work. These tools use cookies and similar technologies, and when you submit a form we share a scrambled (hashed) version of your email, phone number, name, and state with Meta so it can match the request to an ad you saw. We do not share your age, financial goals, or any other financial details with Meta. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings and in your browser’s cookie settings.
